# Mascot Character Bible

## Identity anchor

The canonical character is `public/images/hero-mascot-programmer.png`. Every
story pose must read as the same young green cat guide, not as a new mascot.

Non-negotiable traits:

- Lime-green fur with a pale muzzle and belly
- Dark green ear interiors and tail tip
- Large blue eyes, small pink nose, rounded cheeks
- Dark over-ear developer headphones
- Rounded chibi proportions with a large head and short limbs
- Soft 3D toy rendering, diffuse studio light, and clean transparent edges

## Story arc

The mascot is one traveler moving through six chapters. Props change to explain
the chapter; identity, rendering, camera angle, and emotional warmth stay fixed.

| Chapter | Action | Prop | Narrative purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Top | Working while looking up | Laptop | Introduces the builder |
| About | Explaining a system | Design board | Reveals the thinking process |
| Work | Pointing along a route | Roadmap | Connects constraints to outcomes |
| Experience | Climbing upward | Steps and star | Shows growth over time |
| Skills | Presenting implementation | Code board | Frames tools as supporting actors |
| Contact | Open welcome gesture | Small message cue | Invites the next conversation |

## Composition contract

- Full body and prop must fit inside a square transparent canvas.
- Keep the face within the upper-middle optical zone so crossfades do not jump.
- Light comes from the upper-left with a soft grounding shadow below the feet.
- Props use the portfolio palette: leaf green, cream, charcoal, and restrained
  coral or amber accents.
- Do not add clothing, logos, extra companions, text baked into the image, or a
  different headphone design.

## Runtime behavior

- Desktop uses one persistent stage; pose layers crossfade inside that stage.
- The character travels only during the closing portion of a chapter.
- Dialogue changes at the midpoint of the pose blend so movement and speech feel
  like a single transition.
- Mobile keeps a compact fixed traveler in the lower-right corner. Its bubble is
  clamped to two lines so it remains present without taking over the content.
- Tablet, reduced-motion, and constrained devices use in-flow story checkpoints.
